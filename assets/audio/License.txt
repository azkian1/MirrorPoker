Mirror Poker runtime format adaptation:
The thirteen accompanying Kenney Ogg Vorbis recordings are retained as sources.
The matching WAV runtime files were decoded with FFmpeg to 16-bit little-endian
PCM, preserving their original 44100 Hz sample rate, stereo channels and timing.
No trimming, resampling, normalization or gain adjustment was applied.
The original CC0 license above also applies to these format conversions.
